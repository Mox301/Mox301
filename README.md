#### Research interests

My research asks how a large language model can keep learning after pretraining without forgetting what it already knows, and find the evidence it needs when a question is asked. A model's pretraining, like a student's university, matters less than what comes after it, and I think that work can bring a mid-tier model close to the state of the art. I work on continual learning, information retrieval for search agents, and LLM-as-judge evaluation.
